The menu bar

ClaudeBar lives in your Mac's menu bar as a small cat, with your usage beside it.

You seeIt means
The catThe cat that matters right now: one that needs you first, else one that's working, else one asleep.
5h · 7dHow much of your Claude Code usage limits you've used in the current 5-hour window and the current week, and when each resets.
Hovering the catOpens a peek at the office (see Hover popup).
Where usage is headingUnder the bars, a quiet line says where your pace leads ("On pace for about 55% of the 5-hour limit by 5:40 PM"). It turns amber when you'd reach a limit before it resets, and ClaudeBar warns you once (Settings: Warn before I hit a usage limit). The thin line shows today's use; click the usage area to see the last 7 days.
Clicking the catOpens the dropdown: usage at the top, then the Office, Projects and Settings tabs.

The ? button at the top right of the dropdown opens a short help panel; ☀️ / 🌙 switches ClaudeBar between day and night (it follows your Mac until you pick).

Office

The Office tab shows your Cat Office, one floor at a time, laid out exactly as your own Cat Office lays it out: the same desks, furniture, windows and elevator.

Floors

  • Lounge (floor 1): where idle cats sleep, on the sofa, beds and cushions.
  • Project floors: each project you work on has its own floor; its cats sit at its desks.
  • Desk pool: for work that isn't in a known project.
  • Helpers (sub-agents) sit near their parent cat, or stand beside it when the desks are full.

Picking a floor

  • The floor menu (e.g. "12 claude-bar · 1 cat ⌄") lists every floor; floors with a cat that needs you are marked ●.
  • ▲ ▼ go up or down one floor.
  • The strip under the menu has one bar per floor: green = cats working, amber = a cat needs you, grey = empty; the dark bar is the floor you're on. Click a bar to go there.
  • Follow shows where things are happening: a cat that needs you, else the latest start or finish, else the busiest floor. It turns on each time you open the dropdown; picking a floor yourself turns it off until you click Follow.

Reading the cats

  • Green ring: working. Amber ring or red "!": needs you (a question, a permission, a plan to approve). In the Lounge (napping, reading, on the sofa): idle.
  • Hover a cat for its card: name, status, project, branch, model and what it's doing (e.g. "Coffee break · 4 min left"). The card stays open while you move the pointer onto it, and has two links: Open in Cat Office (Cat Office opens on that cat's panel) and, when ClaudeBar can find it, Show terminal (brings forward the Terminal or iTerm tab the session runs in; macOS asks once to let ClaudeBar do that).
  • Click a row in the list under the room to open Cat Office on that cat.
  • When a cat changes floors it walks to the elevator, rides, and walks out on the other floor.

Answering a cat that needs you

When a cat waits on a permission ("Allow this command?"), a question or a plan, its card and its row in the list show what it's asking, with buttons: Allow, Always allow or Deny; one button per option for a single question; Approve or Reject for a plan. Click one and Cat Office types the answer; the row says Sent, and the cat gets back to work. Several questions, or one with several picks, are answered in Cat Office.

Cat Office can answer sessions running in its Orca terminals, so the buttons show only for those. For others (Terminal, iTerm, VS Code) the question still shows, with "Answer it in its terminal" and the links to get there.

Before typing, Cat Office checks the session still waits on the question you read; if it changed or closed, nothing is typed and the row says so (check it again). If the question isn't on screen in its terminal, or the prompt has no Always allow, the row says that too.

Matching the browser

While a Cat Office page is open, ClaudeBar shows each cat where the browser has it, doing the same thing. Settings → Show spare cats adds the office's own cats too.

With no Cat Office page open, ClaudeBar's idle cats keep busy on their own in the Lounge (naps, books, snacks) by Cat Office's rules, and spare cats roam the whole office; with a page open they follow it exactly.

Hover popup

Hover the menu bar cat to peek at the office without opening the dropdown. It shows the floor Follow would pick, with the same zoom, hover cards and 🎵 button. It closes when you move away. You can turn it off in Settings.

Projects

The Projects tab lists the projects you've added, each with its branch, model, how full its context is, and its status. Add folders from the tab, or edit the list with Settings → Setup & more → Edit config file.

Its first line is today's recap: "4 sessions finished · 2h 10m working · busiest: claude-bar" (since midnight; office cats and terminal sessions alike, helpers counted in their parent's project). It's hidden on a day with nothing yet.

Settings is grouped into General, Alerts and Office, with setup folded into one line at the bottom. Under Setup & more: which Cat Office and how ClaudeBar is connected, Set up ClaudeBar…, Undo ClaudeBar's changes…, Refresh office art checks Cat Office straight away for changed floors, furniture and characters (ClaudeBar also checks by itself about once a minute while the office is open, so an edited character shows up on its own), Edit config file and Open log.

Sounds never pile up: at most one every 3 seconds, and the same cat won't repeat an alert within a minute.

What ClaudeBar changes

ClaudeBar is built to be safe to try: nothing changes until you click Continue, everything is backed up first, and you can undo it all from Settings → Undo ClaudeBar's changes…. See What ClaudeBar changes for every place it touches and how to undo each one.

Using ClaudeBar without Cat Office

ClaudeBar works happily on its own: your usage in the menu bar and your projects, nothing else needed. If you told setup you don't use Cat Office, the Office tab and the hover popup stay out of your way until you do.

Why you might enjoy Cat Office

Cat Office turns each of your Claude sessions into a cat in a little office: typing at its desk while it works, napping or snacking in the Lounge when it's idle, and waving when it needs you. With it, ClaudeBar shows that office right in your menu bar (see Office).

Getting it

Cat Office is a small app your team runs on their own Macs. The easiest way to get it is to ask a teammate who already uses it: they'll be glad to share it and help you get it running. It needs Node.js.

Once it's on your Mac, open Settings → Set up ClaudeBar…. ClaudeBar finds it, and your office appears. There's no rush: come back whenever you like.

If something's wrong

Setting up ClaudeBar (or setting it up again)

The first time ClaudeBar runs, a Set up ClaudeBar window finds your Cat Office, then offers to patch it, show your usage in the menu bar, and start it. Nothing changes until you click Continue on the Connect step. Skip for now (or closing the window) skips it, and it won't open by itself again. To run it again any time, use Settings → Set up ClaudeBar…, or the card at the top of the Office tab ("Cat Office isn't set up yet" or "Some extras are off"). If ClaudeBar replaced your status line, Settings → Restore my old status line puts yours back.

"Cat Office isn't running"

Click Start in the office. If it doesn't start, open your Cat Office folder in Terminal and run npm run dev; ClaudeBar finds it on its own. Settings → Setup & more → Open log shows what happened when ClaudeBar tried to start it.

No usage numbers in the menu bar

The numbers come from Claude Code's status line. Turn on Usage in the menu bar in Settings → Set up ClaudeBar… (or, from the ClaudeBar folder, run scripts/install-statusline.sh), then send any message in Claude Code.

A cat on the wrong floor, or a floor missing

Floors and layouts come from your Cat Office. New floors appear within a minute; reopen the dropdown to refresh. If you edited a room in Cat Office, save it there first.

The cats don't match the browser, or helpers don't show

Those need ClaudeBar's small patch to Cat Office. Tick Patch Cat Office in Settings → Set up ClaudeBar…, then restart Cat Office when it asks.

If the Office tab says Two copies of Cat Office are running, each copy runs its own office, so the browser and ClaudeBar may show different cats. Click Stop the extra one: ClaudeBar stops the copy it isn't reading (sessions running inside that copy end), and tells you which address to open in the browser. Settings → Setup & more shows which copy ClaudeBar reads and whether it's copying your browser page.

"Show terminal" does nothing

The first time, macOS asks to let ClaudeBar control Terminal or iTerm. If you said no, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. "Show terminal" appears only for sessions running in Terminal or iTerm.

The answer buttons don't show, or say Cat Office can't answer

Cat Office can type an answer only into sessions running in Orca. For others, ClaudeBar shows the question with Open in Cat Office and Show terminal, so you can answer it there.

No sound

Check that Settings → Cat sounds is on, then Choose sounds and the volume slider, and your Mac's volume. The finished purr is muted while Focus or Do Not Disturb is on.

No music

Check the 🎵 button (🔇 means off) and the volume slider. The music only plays while the Office tab or the hover popup is open; in the popup it starts after about a second.

ClaudeBar's sounds and music are generated from code, made for ClaudeBar.
